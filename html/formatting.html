HTML Text Formatting Elements:

1. b

This makes the text bold.

2. strong

This makes the text strong and similar to bold.

3. i

This makes the text italic.

4. em

This makes the text emphasized and similar to italic.

5. u

This makes the text underlined.

6. mark

This makes the text highlighted.

7. small

This makes the text small.

8. del

This makes the text defined.

9. ins

This defines inserted texts.

10. sub

This makes the text a subscript.

11. sup

This makes the text a superscript.
